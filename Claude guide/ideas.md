# Ideas for future parts of the guide

Mike may break the guide into smaller pieces that are easier to digest. Below are some ways to split it, plus topics not covered yet. Check with him before drafting any of these.

## Smaller, stand-alone parts (one tab or short doc each)
Each could run 1–2 pages: what it is, one real example with a screenshot, a prompt to copy, and one "watch out for."
1. **Getting Claude for Teachers.** Already in the "Claude" tab; could stand alone as a one-page quick start.
2. **Your first simulation in 15 minutes.** Describe the tricky quiz question, get a sim, and open it on an iPad.
3. **Claude chat vs. Claude Cowork.** When a chat is enough and when Cowork earns its setup time.
4. **Setting up Cowork safely.** A dedicated folder, a separate Windows account if you're on a personal computer, and what it can and can't see.
5. **Putting things on the web.** GitHub Pages and GitHub Desktop, in five minutes.
6. **The instructions file.** How to write down your rules once so every new task follows them. Show a short excerpt of his CLAUDE.md.
7. **Anatomy of a mini-lesson.** The notes sheet, check questions with targeted feedback, the results card, and the teacher view. Use the existing screenshots.
8. **Sub days.** Self-running pages and a short sub plan. The Honors and AP versions differ: Honors sub plans are bare minimum.
9. **Is my class ready for the quiz?** Upload a quiz and get it mapped to what students practiced. Keep stretch questions on purpose.
10. **Turning a bad FRQ into a review.** The block-off-a-ramp example: a simulation plus a step-by-step derivation.
11. **Not just physics.** The kindergarten starter kit, as proof the approach works for any grade.
12. **Tools for your job, not just lessons.** The sub-coverage tracker and the homework checker.
13. **Privacy and school rules.** No student names, keep answer keys private, and check before uploading published or unreleased materials.
14. **Usage limits and staying efficient.** One task per unit, and ask for a sample first.

## Formats that might help
- **A one-page "cheat sheet"** of the best prompts (see `prompts.md`), printable for a department meeting.
- **A 3-minute screen recording** of a mini-lesson on an iPad and the teacher view.
- **An FAQ tab** collecting colleagues' actual questions as they come in.
- **A "Start here" tab** that links to each smaller part in a suggested order.
