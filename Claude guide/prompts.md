# Prompts colleagues can copy

Short prompts that worked for Mike or that follow the same pattern. Tip: describe your students and your constraints first, then ask for one sample before a whole set.

## Getting started
- "I teach [subject] to [grade]. My students use [school iPads / Chromebooks / laptops] and turn work in on [system]. Keep that in mind for everything we make."
- "What are some ways high school [subject] teachers use AI well that don't feel gimmicky?"

## Simulations and lessons
- "A lot of my students missed this quiz question: [paste or attach it]. Make an interactive simulation of that exact situation, with sliders for the variables, so I can show how changing one affects the others. It has to work on an iPad."
- "Here are my slides for tomorrow (attached). I'll be out, and my sub isn't a [subject] teacher. Make a self-running lesson students can do on their iPads, with check questions and a results card they can screenshot."
- "Make one sample first. I'll tell you what to change before you make the rest."
- "Save the format we agreed on as instructions in this folder, so every future lesson follows it."

## Review and assessment
- "Here's my quiz (attached) and what we've practiced so far. Are students ready? Go question by question and show me where each skill was practiced."
- "This FRQ went badly (attached, with the scoring guide). Build a simulation of the situation, with the derivation and an explanation for each part, revealed step by step. Keep it off my public site."

## Putting it online (GitHub)
- "Claude made me an HTML file. Walk me through putting it on a free website with GitHub Pages, one step at a time, and wait for me to finish each step before giving me the next one."
- "Explain the difference between the GitHub website, github.dev, and GitHub Desktop like I'm a teacher, not a programmer."
- "I want Claude to save files straight into my GitHub site so I only have to click a button to publish. How do I set up Claude Cowork and GitHub Desktop to do that?"

## Privacy and setup
- "Before I connect a folder on my computer to Claude, what should I know about privacy, and how should I set it up?"
- "My computer is also my personal computer. Would a separate Windows account for teaching help?"
