# Classroom Starter Kit: start here

This folder gives Claude a head start on making materials for your class. It holds:

- a set of rules for how things should look and work (`CLAUDE.md`)
- a page about your class that Claude fills in with you (`about-my-class.md`)
- tools that build screen activities and printables the same way every time

You don't need to touch any of the code. Claude does that part.

## What it makes
- **Screen activities for the smartboard** that use touch: kids come up and tap to count, choose, or reveal. You move ahead with a tap, the arrow keys, or a presentation remote. They can follow your curriculum's lessons and keep its calm, simple look.
- **Your notes, without the manual.** Each slide has short talking points from your lesson. See them three ways:
  - **📝 Notes:** a quick pop-up on the board.
  - **🖥 Presenter view:** your laptop shows your notes and what's next, while the board shows only the slide. Tap ▶ on either one.
  - **Script card:** a printable page with every slide and its notes.
- **Printables:** one-page worksheets and center sheets, with an answer key when there are answers.
- **A library page** (`my-materials/index.html`) that lists everything you've made, with buttons to open or print.

## Try the examples first
Open the `examples` folder:
- `count-apples.html` opens in your web browser (calm style). Try ▶ and ◀, tapping the apples, 📝 Notes, and 🖥 Presenter. If your smartboard is a separate screen, drag the board window onto it and tap ⛶ Full screen there.
- `count-apples-bright.html` is the same activity in a brighter style, for comparison.
- `index.html` is a sample library page. The "Script card" button shows the printable notes.
- `count-and-circle.pdf` is a printable, and `count-and-circle-KEY.pdf` is its answer key.

## Set up Claude (about 5 minutes)
1. Put this whole folder somewhere you'll keep it, like `Documents\Classroom Starter Kit`.
2. Open the **Claude desktop app** and go to **Cowork**.
3. Make a **project** from this folder (Projects → New project → use an existing folder). Then every task in the project can see these files.
4. Start a task in the project and say:

   > "Read CLAUDE.md and about-my-class.md, then interview me to fill in my class info."

   Claude will ask about your smartboard setup, the style you like, and where your curriculum files are.

5. After that, just ask for what you need. For example:
   - "Here's Unit 2, Lesson 5 (PDF attached). Make a board version with my talking points on each slide, and add a tap activity where it fits."
   - "Make a screen activity for the letter B: things that start with B, and a sound-it-out game."
   - "Make a printable for counting to 10 with a fall theme, plus an answer key."
   - "Plan next week's math: one screen activity a day and two printables."
   - "Combine this week's printables into one PDF so I can print it all at once."

## Good to know
- **Your curriculum files.** Using your school's materials to prepare your own lessons is the kind of use most curriculum licenses expect. Still, your district's license is what counts, so check with your school if you're unsure. These files stay on your computer, not online. Claude summarizes the scripts into short talking points instead of copying them. In Claude's settings, under Privacy, you can choose whether your chats are used to improve Claude.
- Everything opens without internet.
- Claude asks before changing anything you already have.
- If you work on more than one computer, keep this folder in a synced place (Google Drive for desktop, OneDrive, or similar).
