Counting Apples

How many apples?

Let's count together!

Say: "How many apples do you think there are?"

  • Take 2–3 guesses before counting.
  • Tap ▶, press →, or click your remote to go on.

Tap each apple as we count

We counted 0

Say: "Let's count together. Touch each apple only once."

  • Invite one child to the board to tap while everyone counts out loud.
  • Look for one tap = one number.
  • "↺ Count again" starts over for the next helper.

Which number shows how many apples?

Say: "Which number tells how many apples?"

  • Fingers up first, then a helper taps.
  • Answer: 7.

5 apples and 2 more

+

5 + 2 = 7

Say: "Here are 5 apples. Now 2 more come."

  • Each ▶ adds one piece: the plus, the 2 apples, then the number sentence.
  • ◀ takes the last piece away.

Show me 7 on your fingers!

7

Say: "Show me 7 on your fingers!"

  • Scan for 5 on one hand and 2 on the other.
